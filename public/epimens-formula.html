The Epimens formula

Seven botanicals, one capsule.

Horny goat weed

Epimedium leaf, the natural source of icariin, which supports healthy blood flow.

Maca root

A Peruvian root studied for its support of sexual desire and energy.

Panax ginseng

A classic Korean root traditionally used for stamina and vitality.

Saw palmetto

Berries of a small fan palm, widely used to support prostate health.

Polypodium vulgare

A traditional European fern used to support overall vitality.

Muira puama

Amazonian “potency wood”, traditionally used to support desire and mood.

Longjack root

Tongkat ali from Southeast Asia, studied for strength and healthy testosterone levels.
