How does Posterity work?

Posterity is built on Tribulus terrestris extract standardized to 45% saponins, the active plant compounds that drive its effects. Multiple clinical trials show that taking Tribulus extract for 1 to 3 months significantly improves scores on the Female Sexual Function Index, and it supports sexual desire and satisfaction in both men and women.

How do I use it?

Take 1–2 capsules daily. Studies on Tribulus extract looked at 1 to 3 months of regular use, so take it consistently.

Can I take Posterity with Epimens?

Yes. Posterity and Epimens are built on different ingredients, so they cover different sides of sexual health. Posterity’s Tribulus terrestris has been studied for sexual desire and satisfaction, while Epimens’ horny goat weed and icariin have been studied for sexual desire and blood flow. Taken together, they give you support from both angles.
